# Evidence: subscription-pricing

Delta only. Shared digit, component, "$0", and loyalty findings are not repeated here.

## Flat rate above pay-per-use as the plan, not just an add-on

**Rule.** Cover what most members want in a flat price set above the pay-per-use equivalent; keep metered pricing as entry.
**Effect.** Purchasing professionals preferred a $6,000 fixed fee to $50/h × 100 h, including at a premium.
**Why.** People overestimate usage and pay to cap the meter.
**Source.** See `pricing` — Kienzler, Kowalkowski & Kindström (2021), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2021.04.024

## Same left digit if you want upgrades

**Rule.** Avoid a just-below price on the cheaper tier when you want people to step up.
**Source.** See `pricing` (left-digit / upgrade-path finding).

## Perk with a minimum; auto-enroll rewards

**Rule.** A paid membership perk without a minimum fragments orders; enroll everyone in one threshold reward.
**Source.** See `post-purchase` (Guo & Liu 2023; Gopalakrishnan et al. 2021).
